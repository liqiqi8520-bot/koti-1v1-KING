Two Event Lanes

Barangay courts build trust. Mall venues build showcase value.

KOTI should separate community penetration from premium venue activation. Local sponsors can use barangay stops to reach harder-to-enter communities, while mall and venue partners can turn the event into a visible public spectacle.

Barangay Court Route Community-first reach

Best for local brands, city partners, public programs, telco, FMCG, and services that want direct access to players, families, and neighborhood crowds.

Mall / Venue Showcase High-visibility activation

Best for sponsor booths, product demos, livestream setups, creator appearances, and brand-safe photo moments in locations such as SM MOA, SM Seaside, or BGC Market! Market! style venues.
