Season History

From 121 community games to core-city qualifiers.

Season 1 121 Community Games

Season 1 promoted the format through 121 barangay-level games, split across 60 North and 61 South communities, using local winners to create finals momentum.

Season 2 Core-City Qualifiers

Season 2 upgrades the model into larger city qualifiers. Seven stops are completed so far, reaching around 70 barangays, with a wider 170+ community roadmap still expanding.

Future University + New Regions

North and South university circuits, Mindanao, Palawan, Davao, and other priority regions remain in the growth plan.

Impact Proof
